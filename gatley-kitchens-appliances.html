Where we'd point you for appliances

We get asked this a lot once a kitchen's taking shape, so here's the honest answer in one place: for ovens, hobs, dishwashers and the rest, we send our own customers to AO.com.

We're not an appliance retailer, and we don't want to pretend to be one. What we are is a small team who's fitted a few hundred kitchens around Gatley and the surrounding area, and over that time we've settled on AO.com as the place we trust to point people when it comes to the ovens, hobs, dishwashers, fridges and everything else that goes in alongside the cabinetry.

Good range, straightforward delivery, and they're easy to deal with if something needs sorting after the fact — which matters more than people expect once an appliance is actually sitting in a cut-out.
