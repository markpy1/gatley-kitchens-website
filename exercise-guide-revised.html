Dead Bug
Core Stability — Anti-Tilt
Bodyweight
3×10 each
sets × reps (per side)
Lie on back, arms straight up toward ceiling, hips and knees 90°. Lower right arm overhead + left leg straight — stop 2 inches above ground. Return, repeat opposite side. Lower back MUST stay flat on floor. This fixes anterior pelvic tilt.
📈 THE most important posture exercise. If your lower back lifts, you've gone too far — scale back. Slow, controlled, lower back NEVER leaves floor.

Legs + Glutes

Wednesday — Glute-Dominant Lower Body

40–45 mins · Barbell · Machines · Dumbbells
Tip: Leg training raises testosterone and growth hormone. Strong glutes fix anterior pelvic tilt. Hip thrust is your posture priority — load it heavy and squeeze hard at the top.
STRONG GLUTES = BETTER POSTURE + FIXED PELVIC TILT + MORE FAT BURNED
Barbell Hip Thrust
Glutes — PRIORITY #1
Bench + Barbell + Pad
4×12
sets × reps
Upper back on bench, barbell across hips with pad. Feet flat, shoulder width. Drive hips up by SQUEEZING GLUTES HARD at the top — 1 second pause, squeeze like you're trying to crack walnuts. Lower slowly 2 seconds. Ribs down, core tight — no lower back hyperextension.
📈 This is the single best posterior chain exercise and directly fixes anterior pelvic tilt. You're at 20kg — aim to add 5kg every 2-3 weeks. Real glute activation only happens with proper load.

Thursday — Back, Rear Delts, Biceps, Core

40–45 mins · Barbell · Dumbbells · Cable
SCAPULAR PULL: Every back movement — think about pulling shoulder blades DOWN and BACK. This counteracts rounded shoulders.
Barbell Row
Back — Primary
Barbell
4×8
sets × reps
Feet shoulder-width, knees soft. Bar at mid-shin. Chest up, shoulders back. Drive elbows to hips, pull bar to chest/lower ribcage. Squeeze shoulder blades hard at the top — hold 1 second. Lower with control. Back stays neutral — no rounding.
📈 The best antidote to rounded shoulders. Track weight session to session — add 2.5kg when you hit 4×8 cleanly.

Dumbbells

sets × reps (per arm)
