How Much Does a New Kitchen Cost in South Manchester?

An honest breakdown of what affects the price, and what to watch out for when comparing quotes.

By Mark from Gatley Kitchens

Ask three kitchen firms and you'll probably get three different answers, usually starting with "it depends". That's true, but it doesn't help much when you're trying to plan. This guide covers what shapes the cost of a new kitchen in South Manchester, so you know what to think about before you speak to anyone.

What our kitchens typically cost

We most commonly provide kitchens between £10,000 and £20,000, fitted. Where your project lands in that range depends on the factors below.

Why the price varies

  • Size and layout. A single-wall kitchen costs less than an open-plan room with an island.
  • Units. Flat-pack, rigid and bespoke cabinets sit at very different price points.
  • Worktops. Laminate is the most affordable option. Quartz and granite cost considerably more.
  • Appliances. Integrated ovens, hobs and fridges can add a significant amount, depending on the brand. Check whether it's cheaper to buy them directly, or whether the kitchen company has discounts it can pass on.

What our quote includes

Our quote includes removing your existing kitchen and fitting the new one, including units and worktops. Any work outside that scope will be listed separately in your quote, so you know exactly what you're paying for.

Keeping costs sensible

  • Think about how you actually use your kitchen and what would give you the most benefit.
  • Keep plumbing and gas where they are if you can. Moving them adds cost quickly.
  • Ask for an itemised quote, not a single figure, so you can compare like with like.
  • Be cautious about paying large sums upfront.

Frequently asked questions

Most installations take around 5 to 10 working days, depending on the size and complexity of the job.
